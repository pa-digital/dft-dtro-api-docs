Do School and Emergency Service Station KEEP CLEAR zig-zag markings outside of London covered by a TRO need to be submitted to D-TRO?
=====================================================================================================================================

Yes, if they are covered by a TRO they will be required to be sent to D-TRO.

Please note that even if certain types of traffic restrictions are not covered by a TRO the information relating to them may still be sent to D-TRO on a voluntary basis.
