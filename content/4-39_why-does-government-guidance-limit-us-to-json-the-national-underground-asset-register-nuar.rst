Why does government guidance limit us to JSON? The National Underground Asset Register (NUAR) for example is a DSIT project and this will accept supply in any GIS file format.
===============================================================================================================================================================================

Cabinet Office guidance prefers the use of JSON as a data format. This does not exclude the potential use of GeoJSON, which is a derivative of JSON. See also:

https://www.gov.uk/guidance/gds-api-technical-and-data-standards#build-your-api
