Is it correct that D-TRO does not show definitive legal TROs? Is any information missing, such as exemptions? Would Google Maps etc need a disclaimer?
======================================================================================================================================================

Yes, D-TROs are a digital extract.ll of the TRO conditions and exemptions are required to be incorporated in each individual D-TRO. This is addressed in Issue 47 on our GitHub:

https://github.com/department-for-transport-public/D-TRO/issues/47

It is for third party data consumers to determine is and what disclaimers to provide for their service.
