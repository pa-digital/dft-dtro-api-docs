Regarding TTRO, we are not always informed when they are lifted, we can't rely on permit start stops as often the TTRO does not run for the entire works permit period, how will this be managed?
=================================================================================================================================================================================================

The ability to report starts/stops is built into the D-TRO Service.

There are various options and ways that this could be introduced. Permit information for Streetworks and Roadworks seems a good starting point. Street Manager has the ability to record the current traffic management status as well as the highest traffic management (Streetworks has the concept of lowest to highest traffic management with a road closure being the highest and a lane closure the next highest etc). A link between the D-TRO and Street Manager services, using that functionality, is being considered.
