Are on-street parking TROs in scope of D-TRO and why are the RTRA 1984 sections that cover on-street parking places (s32, 45, etc) missing from the Automated Vehicles (AV) Act 2024?
=====================================================================================================================================================================================

Please refer to the D-TRO Consultation including the draft Traffic Orders and Notices (Provision of Digital Information) (England) Regulations 2025 for full details of which types of orders are in scope of the D-TRO Service.

Please note that even if certain types of traffic restrictions are not covered by a TRO the information relating to them may still be sent to D-TRO on a voluntary basis.
