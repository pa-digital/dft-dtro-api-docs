Should Proposed Static TROs be sent to D-TRO? If so, what is the reason these are required as they may or may not be sealed?
============================================================================================================================

Yes. This is explained in detail in the consultation which ended on 21 March 2025 (see 1.3). In summary, the information about proposed orders is required to:

allow for consultation on permanent and speed limit orders
give notice for temporary traffic regulation orders
support consultations; and
support information sharing
