For Emergency TTRNs / TTROs, we often don't get the TTRO application straight away,. What do we do in this instance?
====================================================================================================================

The D-TRO Service is capable of receiving Emergency TTRNs/TTROs and any of these may be published to the service if they exist. In other words, if you write a TTRN out, it needs to be sent into the D-TRO service. If you don’t write it, you can’t send it.
