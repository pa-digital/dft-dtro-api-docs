How will TRAs that use their own in-house systems for digitising TROs be able to join ? What is expected of those TRAs that don’t have the standard support of a DSP?
=====================================================================================================================================================================

The D-TRO service is agnostic to type of software that is used to generate and publish digital TROs. We will accept digital TROs from DSPs and from TRAs directly. All submissions to the D-TRO service will need to comply with the D-TRO Data Specification.
