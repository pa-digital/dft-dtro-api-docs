My question is whether 'old' Orders can/should be simply added to our digital s provider (i.e. digital representation of text Orders) or whether it is considered best to consolidate into new dedicated digital Orders?
========================================================================================================================================================================================================================

It is acceptable to either send in the digital extract based on the order or to make a new order with an accompanying D-TRO. We are aware that the latter option might be preferable to certain TRAs given that it would ensure that the TRO better matches what is in place  in terms of traffic signs and road markings.
